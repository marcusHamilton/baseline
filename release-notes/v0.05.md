Baseline can now update itself, plus a round of layout, accessibility and speed fixes.

## New
- One-tap updates: Today and Settings offer a new release and install it in place
- A short "What's new" note, like this one, the first time you open an updated app
- Check for updates daily, or tap Check now in Settings
## Improved
- Today's dials fit narrow phones and large text sizes without squeezing or clipping
- Trends charts show the start date and Today under each chart
- The Calendar legend wraps instead of being cut off
- TalkBack can jump between screen, section and card titles
- Syncing and score updates are faster, and screens refresh less during a first sync
## Fixed
- Sleep bed and wake times follow your time zone when it changes
- The keyboard no longer covers "Save and test" in onboarding and Settings
- Status and navigation bars now look the same on Android 10 to 14
- Today says "Strain target" on past days instead of "Strain target today"
