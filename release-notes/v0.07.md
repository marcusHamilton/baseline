An optional AI assistant for your health data, a tidier Settings, and a week load bar.

## New
- Ask button opens an AI chat about the page you are on, on-device or with your own key
- Short AI insights on Today, Sleep and each activity, refreshed when your data changes
- Settings has an AI assistant page: Off, On-device only, Cloud only or Hybrid
- Week load bar on Activities and Trends shows this week's load against a normal week
- Settings has a Privacy & data page showing what leaves your phone
## Improved
- Settings is one short list, and each topic opens on its own page
- Pulling down on Today also checks for a new release
